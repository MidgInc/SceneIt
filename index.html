Security & Privacy

Your identity stays yours

SceneIt uses a zero-knowledge architecture: we can verify your evidence is authentic and unaltered without ever knowing who you are.

We collect only what's cryptographically necessary. Sensitive actions, account recovery, identity verification, legal export, are handled exclusively in the mobile app, with end-to-end protections.
